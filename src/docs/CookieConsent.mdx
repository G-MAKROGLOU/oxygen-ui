# CookieConsent

A cookie / consent banner for accepting or rejecting non-essential cookies and
linking to your policy.

## Mental model

**Self-managing by default.** It remembers the visitor's choice in
`localStorage` under `storageKey` (default `'oxygen-cookie-consent'`) and stays
dismissed on return. Drop it once near your app root and you're done. Wire
`onAccept` / `onDecline` to (de)activate analytics. For full control, pass
`storageKey={null}` and drive visibility with `open`. Rendered in a Portal,
fixed to the bottom.

## Quick start

```tsx
import { CookieConsent } from '@geomak/ui'

<CookieConsent
  title="Cookies"
  message="We use cookies to enhance your experience."
  declineLabel="Reject"              // omit for accept-only
  learnMoreHref="/privacy"
  onAccept={enableAnalytics}
  onDecline={disableAnalytics}
/>
```

Read the stored choice yourself anytime via `localStorage.getItem('oxygen-cookie-consent')`
(`'accepted'` | `'declined'`).
